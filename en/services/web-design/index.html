How we work

  1. Discovery

    We clarify the need, goals, users and constraints together, and put the scope and budget in writing.

  2. Design

    We design screen flows and the interface, and plan the architecture and any hardware. Nothing gets built without your approval.

  3. Development

    We work in two-week cycles and show you a working version at the end of each one.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, monitor, train your team and keep the system up to date under a maintenance agreement.

Frequently asked questions

How long until a company site is live?
Two to four weeks if content is ready; a little longer if we write it together.
Can we update the site ourselves?
We set up a content panel if you need one, or handle updates under a maintenance agreement.
Can you redesign our current site?
Yes. We redirect old addresses to the new pages so you keep your search rankings.
