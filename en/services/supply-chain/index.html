Supply chain support

Purchasing support: the right product, on time

We help businesses find what they need from the right supplier, at the right price and on time, and coordinate the import, export and logistics steps.

Request a quote

Purchasing is invisible in most businesses yet affects cost more than almost anything. Done systematically, supplier selection, price comparison and delivery tracking save both money and time.

SourcingPurchasingImportExportLogisticsProcurement software
  • Supplier research: domestic and international alternatives compared
  • Purchasing: collecting quotes, negotiating and tracking orders
  • Import and export: documents, customs and transport coordinated
  • Logistics: transport and delivery planning
  • Digital process: purchase requests and approvals moved into software

How we work

  1. Discovery

    We clarify the need, goals, users and constraints together, and put the scope and budget in writing.

  2. Design

    We design screen flows and the interface, and plan the architecture and any hardware. Nothing gets built without your approval.

  3. Development

    We work in two-week cycles and show you a working version at the end of each one.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, monitor, train your team and keep the system up to date under a maintenance agreement.
