Agricultural technology

Farming that measures the soil and saves water

We bring electronics, software and AI to agriculture, building every layer ourselves, from sensors that collect field data to apps that turn it into decisions.

Request a quote

Most growing decisions still rely on guesswork: when to irrigate, how much fertiliser, when disease will strike. With regular field data these questions get measured answers, and water and input costs fall.

IoTLoRa / GSMSensorsCircuit boardsAIMobile app
  • IoT: wireless sensor networks for soil moisture, temperature, climate and nutrients
  • Smart equipment: irrigation, spraying and greenhouse automation driven by data
  • AI: yield prediction and early warning for disease and pests
  • Mobile apps: growers see field status and get alerts on their phone
  • Electronics: circuit boards, sensor integration and prototyping

End to end

Sensors, connectivity, cloud and app come from one team, so the pieces fit.

Low power

Designed to run in the field for long periods on battery or solar.

In the grower's language

Apps are simple enough to use without technical knowledge.

R&D partnerships

Open to joint projects with universities, machinery makers and public programmes.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    We clarify the need, goals, users and constraints together, and put the scope and budget in writing.

  2. Design

    We design screen flows and the interface, and plan the architecture and any hardware. Nothing gets built without your approval.

  3. Development

    We work in two-week cycles and show you a working version at the end of each one.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, monitor, train your team and keep the system up to date under a maintenance agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Do you have a ready product?
Our agritech work is at the R&D stage. We're open to pilots and joint development.
Do you work with machinery manufacturers?
Yes, adding sensors, electronics and software to agricultural machinery.
Can we run a publicly funded project?
Agritech projects are good candidates for KOSGEB and TÜBİTAK programmes. See R&D and grants.
