IoT solutions

IoT solutions: systems that measure your equipment and make it talk

From sensors and embedded electronics to the cloud, dashboards and mobile apps, we build every layer of the Internet of Things with one team.

Request a quote

Machines, warehouses, cold rooms, meters and field equipment produce data every day, and most of it never reaches anyone. With IoT, readings are monitored live, alerts arrive when a threshold is crossed, and maintenance and energy decisions rest on measurement instead of guesswork.

IoTSensorsEmbedded systemsLoRa / GSMCircuit boardsRemote monitoring
  • Sensing and data collection: temperature, humidity, energy use, vibration, level and location
  • Embedded systems and electronics: circuit board design, sensor integration and prototyping
  • Connectivity: wireless transmission over LoRa, GSM, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
  • Cloud and dashboards: web dashboards that collect, report and raise alerts
  • Mobile apps: device status on your phone, notifications and remote control

End to end

Sensor hardware, firmware, cloud and app come from one team, so the pieces fit.

Low power

Field devices are designed to run for long periods on battery or solar.

Integration

Collected data can flow into your ERP, accounting or Ekipsis.

R&D partnerships

Open to joint projects with universities, manufacturers and public programmes.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    We clarify the need, goals, users and constraints together, and put the scope and budget in writing.

  2. Design

    We design screen flows and the interface, and plan the architecture and any hardware. Nothing gets built without your approval.

  3. Development

    We work in two-week cycles and show you a working version at the end of each one.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, monitor, train your team and keep the system up to date under a maintenance agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Which devices can be connected?
Almost any device that produces a measurable value, such as machines, warehouses, cold rooms, meters and field equipment, can take a sensor and communication module. We assess fit together during discovery.
Do you work with manufacturers?
Yes, with manufacturers who want to add sensors, electronics and software to their products.
Can we run a publicly funded project?
IoT and embedded projects are good candidates for KOSGEB and TÜBİTAK programmes. See R&D and grants.
