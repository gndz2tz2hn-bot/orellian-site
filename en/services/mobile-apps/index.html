Mobile apps

Mobile app development: your business, in your pocket

We build iOS and Android apps for your field team, customers or dealers from a single codebase, and take them all the way to the stores.

Request a quote

A mobile app can't be designed apart from its admin panel. We design the app together with the panel and API behind it, so data entered in the field shows up at headquarters instantly.

React NativeExpoiOSAndroidPush notificationsOffline mode
  • Field apps: service forms with photos, location and signatures
  • Customer apps: orders, bookings, loyalty and notifications
  • Internal apps: staff, approvals and communication
  • Store publishing: App Store and Google Play accounts, review and release
  • Offline mode: works in the field without losing data

One codebase, two platforms

React Native ships iOS and Android from the same code, halving maintenance.

Notifications

Push notifications reach the right person at the right moment.

Location and camera

GPS, camera, QR and NFC are wired into the workflow.

Privacy compliant

Explicit consent and data-protection rules are built in for sensitive data like location.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    We clarify the need, goals, users and constraints together, and put the scope and budget in writing.

  2. Design

    We design screen flows and the interface, and plan the architecture and any hardware. Nothing gets built without your approval.

  3. Development

    We work in two-week cycles and show you a working version at the end of each one.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, monitor, train your team and keep the system up to date under a maintenance agreement.

Frequently asked questions

Do you publish the app to the stores?
Yes. We handle everything from developer accounts to store review; the accounts are in your name.
Do we pay separately for iOS and Android?
No. With one codebase, both platforms are part of the same project.
Will there be a web admin panel?
If you need one, we build it in the same project.
