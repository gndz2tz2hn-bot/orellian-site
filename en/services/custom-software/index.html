Custom software

Custom software, written around your business

Off-the-shelf packages force you into their mould. We do the opposite: we listen to how you work and build software from scratch that speeds it up and cuts errors.

Request a quote

Work that runs on spreadsheets, email threads and paper forms eventually holds growth back. Custom software brings it into one system: data is entered once and everyone who needs it sees it at the right moment.

Web applicationREST APIPostgreSQLNode.js / NestJSReact / Next.jsCloud or on-premise
  • Internal applications: orders, inventory, production, purchasing, service and approval flows
  • Integrations: ERP, accounting, e-invoicing, shipping, banking and marketplace connections
  • Reporting dashboards: scattered data brought together on one screen
  • Automation: scheduled jobs and notifications that remove repetitive work
  • API development: so your systems talk to each other and to your partners

Your process, your screens

Screens are designed around how your users actually work: no extra fields, no extra clicks.

You own the code

Source code and documentation are handed over to you under contract.

Grows with you

A modular architecture lets new needs be added without breaking what works.

Security from day one

Permissions, audit logs and data-protection compliance are handled at design time.

How we work

  1. Discovery

    We clarify the need, goals, users and constraints together, and put the scope and budget in writing.

  2. Design

    We design screen flows and the interface, and plan the architecture and any hardware. Nothing gets built without your approval.

  3. Development

    We work in two-week cycles and show you a working version at the end of each one.

  4. Launch and support

    We launch, monitor, train your team and keep the system up to date under a maintenance agreement.

Frequently asked questions

How long does custom software take?
It depends on scope. A small internal tool takes 4–8 weeks; multi-module systems can take a few months. After discovery we give you a phased timeline.
Can it work with the software we already use?
Yes. We integrate with most ERP, accounting and e-invoicing systems via APIs or data exchange.
Do you provide support after launch?
Under a maintenance agreement we handle bug fixes, updates and small improvements.
