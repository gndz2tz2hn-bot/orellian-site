R&D projects and public funding

We plan, run and report our innovative projects to fit public funding programmes, and work as the software, electronics or IoT partner for businesses running funded projects.

KOSGEB

Programmes for SMEs' product development, innovation and digital transformation projects.

  • R&D, product development and innovation support
  • Digital transformation and technology investment
  • Entrepreneurship support

TÜBİTAK

TEYDEB programmes fund technically deep industrial R&D projects.

  • 1501 Industrial R&D Projects
  • 1507 SME R&D Start-up
  • University–industry collaboration

Ministry of Industry and Technology

Incentives for R&D, design and domestic technology production.

  • R&D and design centre incentives
  • Technology development zone benefits
  • Investment incentives

Application windows, funding rates and conditions change often. Check the agency's official site for current details, and we'll shape the project around them together.

What we do in a project

  1. Idea and fit

    We assess which programme suits the idea, in terms of novelty and technical risk.

  2. Project design

    We write up work packages, timeline, budget items and technical goals.

  3. Development

    We build the software, electronics and IoT work packages as technical partner.

  4. Reporting

    We prepare technical outputs, test results and documentation for periodic reports.

Our R&D focus

  • Internet of Things (IoT): sensor networks, remote monitoring, connected devices
  • Business software: multi-tenant SaaS, mobile field apps
  • Embedded systems and electronics: circuit design, sensor integration
  • AI: image processing, forecasting models, decision support

Let's talk about your project

Whether it's a website, an end-to-end software system or a publicly funded R&D project, we'll clarify the need together in the first call and give you a roadmap and an estimated budget.
