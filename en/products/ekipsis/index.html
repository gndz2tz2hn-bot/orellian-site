SaaS · Business management platform

Ekipsis: your team's operating system

Cloud software that brings a business's daily work into one platform, from clocking in and leave to payroll, purchasing and field service. With a web panel and a mobile app.

Visit the siteContact

Clock-in the way that suits you

Each company picks its method: location or company Wi-Fi verification in the mobile app, ready-made RFID card terminals, or scanning a QR code at the entrance. Location check-in requires the employee's explicit consent; employees who don't consent verify via Wi-Fi.

Only the modules you need

Time and attendance is standard; other modules are added per company, so you never pay for features you don't use.

You define the roles

Each company sets its own roles and permission levels to match its organisation, and approval flows follow that structure.
