Custom software or an off-the-shelf package? 6 questions to ask first

Published: October 31, 2026

When a business decides to go digital, the first question is usually: buy a ready-made program, or have custom software built? Both are right in some situations. These six questions help you see which one is right for you.

1. How different is your process from your industry's standard?

Accounting, e-invoicing or basic inventory look similar everywhere, and packages for them are mature. But if the way you work is what sets you apart, forcing it into a package usually breaks it.

2. How much would you need to customise the package?

Small settings are fine. But if every new need means another module, licence or consulting fee, the total can exceed custom software.

3. How many people will use it, on which devices?

With per-user licences, cost grows with your team. If your field staff use phones, test the package's mobile support properly.

4. Does it need to talk to your other systems?

If you exchange data with ERP, accounting, e-commerce or machines, integration capability is decisive. A closed package can make it hard to get your data out.

5. Who will own the data and the code?

With a package, data lives in the vendor's system and the product's future is their call. With custom software, code and data can belong to you by contract.

6. How soon do you need it working?

A package can be live in days. Custom software takes weeks or months, though with phased development the first modules can be in use quickly.

In short

If your process is standard and you want to start fast, a package makes sense. If your process is your competitive edge, needs many integrations, or you want to escape licence costs long term, custom software is the better investment.
