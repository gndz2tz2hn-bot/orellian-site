Bringing technology to businesses of every size

Orellian was founded in 2026 in Kırklareli, Türkiye. We combine software, electronics and trade to build products that make businesses' work easier.

Orellian started from a simple observation: small and medium businesses either can't reach the technology large companies use, or are stuck with expensive packages that don't fit how they work.

So we work in two directions. We build our own products, like Ekipsis, that businesses of any size can use, and we run custom software, website and mobile app projects for businesses.

The Internet of Things is the R&D area we care about most. We combine sensors, embedded electronics and software so businesses can measure and remotely manage their equipment.
